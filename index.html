My dog Ellie is a Camp Mikey regular, and at this point they're more like friends than a daycare.

A few times a year, I have to travel, and Ellie ends up staying with them for a couple of weeks at a time. Not once have they made me feel like I was asking for too much. They just say yes, and then send me photos of Ellie living her best life.

And Ellie? Every week she comes back home so happy. Completely wrecked, too. She plays so hard all day that she comes home starving, eats her dinner in about four seconds, then passes out and doesn't move until morning. I've started to suspect she likes it there more than she likes her house, and honestly, fair enough.

What gets me is that it isn't just a service. They've built this genuinely magical little world for the dogs, and they love every single one of them like their own. That's not something you can fake, and you can see it in the dogs when they walk in the door.

So here's my position: every dog deserves at least one Camp Mikey day a week. Not just Ellie. Every single dog in Ireland. I'm willing to campaign on this.
